Our story

Ember & Oak began in 2019 with a secondhand roaster, a borrowed garage, and a stubborn belief that coffee tastes better when someone cares about every bean. Seven years later we still roast in small batches, still taste every roast before it ships, and still believe a great cup of coffee can rescue an ordinary morning.

Our coffee comes from twelve family farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. We pay above fair-trade rates, roast light enough to honor each origin, and print the roast date on every bag. When you brew our coffee at home, you are tasting the farm, the altitude, and the week it was roasted. That freshness is the whole point of a local roastery, and it is why our regulars plan their mornings around us.

The menu
